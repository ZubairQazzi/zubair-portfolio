Research & capstone

Can historical search signals help prioritize content review?

My FlyRank ML capstone evaluates whether historical impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, and search data coverage can help rank published content pages for human refresh review.

The result is deliberately framed as decision support, not automation: the model helps decide where a reviewer should investigate first.
